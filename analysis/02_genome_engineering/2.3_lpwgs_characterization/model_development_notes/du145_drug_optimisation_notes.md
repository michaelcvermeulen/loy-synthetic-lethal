## Source and reproducible calculation

[Frozen export, source values and comparisons](../../figures/chapter3/source_data/du145_drug_optimisation/) were generated by [process_du145_drug_optimisation.py](process_du145_drug_optimisation.py). The raw path and input/script checksums are recorded in `provenance.json`.

The original run contains 24 target/reference well pairs: six treatment conditions × three targets, plus three targets each for sgCTRL and a separate specimen labelled LOY pass1. All are marked Manual in the instrument export; no regating was performed. Each treatment/target has one observation. The edited master table records CP + Q53 + P47 and one nucleofection for drug-treated samples, but exact population lineages and links to later sequencing are not yet established.

Ratios were independently calculated from target and reference concentrations and checked against the instrument Ratio field. Reduction = 100 × (1 − treatment ratio/vehicle ratio), using the DMSO condition in this run for each target. No reference-copy conversion, historical-control pooling, negative-value clipping or significance testing was performed.

Vehicle ratios are approximately 0.068853 for AMELY/AMELX, approximately 0.051275 for DDX3Y/RNaseP and 0.045333 for KDM5D/RNaseP; full precision is retained in the CSVs. These percentages quantify changes in concentration ratios, not Y-negative cell fractions. Differences across loci preclude summarising them as a single whole-chromosome loss estimate. RPS4Y1 was not included, so this experiment does not measure depletion at that retained distal-Yp locus.

## Interpretation and remaining work

Panobinostat-containing conditions had lower ratios than vehicle at all three measured loci, but effect sizes varied. KU-55933 provided no consistent additional benefit across doses and loci. This is descriptive evidence from one measurement per condition/target, not replicated efficacy or a dose-response model. No recovery, viability, treatment-selection or whole-chromosome-loss outcome was inferred.

Primary pharmacology references checked for the introductory rationale: [Hickson et al. (2004), KU-55933](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15604286/) and [panobinostat characterisation in thoracic tumour models](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3605895/). These support inhibitor identity and pharmacology, not the mechanism of the observed DU145 dosage changes.

## Biological replication clarified, 23 September 2026

The author confirmed that each of the six treatment conditions was an independent biological replicate of nucleofection. The grouping by panobinostat exposure therefore contains two independent nucleofections without panobinostat (DMSO and KU-55933 alone) and four with panobinostat (30 or 75 nM, each with or without KU-55933). This supersedes earlier statements that independent nucleofection replication was not established. It does not assign additional replicate counts within each individual treatment condition or establish DNA-sample matching between assays.
