# Section 3.4.6 figure-panel citation audit

Audited 25 September 2026. Scope: all selected main and supplementary figures assigned to 3.4.6, checked against the active Results, central captions and actual panel lettering in each selected SVG, with PDF text extraction for every figure. The composite reference-profile and treatment figures were also inspected visually. No quantitative reanalysis or full source-data validation was performed.

## Outcome

- 13 active figures: 8 main and 5 supplementary.
- 42 plotted units: 41 lettered panels and one unlettered single-panel figure.
- Before: 9 lettered panels had explicit narrative citations; the unlettered H2452 figure was correctly cited. All 13 figures had a figure-level link, although the A549/DU145 profile link was a standalone stub.
- After citation-only edits: 32 lettered panels have explicit citations, plus the unlettered H2452 figure. Nine panels still need discussion or a decision to omit them.

- Added letters only where existing text discusses the evidence; moved the standalone A549 profile link into its existing introductory sentence. Repeated assay citations beside the two-round results. No new scientific claims or values added.

## Content and citation issues retained for later

- **Composite profile coverage:** HT1080 S01a and A549/DU145 S01a are cited for cell-line characteristics only. This does not mean all unlettered content within those composites is discussed. The A549 public Y-copy-number/RNA discrepancy, selective dependencies and public genome-wide profile remain unaddressed. DU145 S01b remains uncited entirely.
- **Section-opening dependency rationale (Results L63):** it names DDX3X, EIF1AX, RPS4X and ZFX, whereas the selected reference profiles show top selective dependencies. Do not attach these panels as if they directly demonstrate that four-gene comparison. Identify the matching evidence or revise later.
- **HT1080/A549 regional RNA panels:** the cited e panels also include PAR genes; the 3.4.6 text discusses male-specific genes only. A549 PAR findings appear in 3.4.7.2, but this does not cover the other lines or their specific e-panel PAR points.
- **DU145 distal-Yp supplementary panel b:** the main distal retained interval is covered, but the separate 8.3?8.9 Mb retained-signal bracket visible in the figure is not discussed. Decide whether it warrants text, an explicit qualification, or a display change; do not imply the figure shows only one retained interval.
- **Treatment F01c,d:** letters are already cited, but the prose lists raw whole-Y/autosome ratios whereas d displays percentage depletion. Panel c displays regional relative copy number, not raw whole-Y depth ratios. The raw numbers need an appropriate source/table pointer; adding letters alone does not fix the mismatch.
- **Treatment F01d:** grouped male-specific Yp and euchromatic Yq depletion medians are plotted but the grouped-results sentence discusses only whole-Y coverage. These subplots remain under-described despite the panel-d citation.
- **Mixing validation wording:** the Results says two DNA concentrations; the selected panels are 50 and 100 ng total DNA input per reaction. Flagged, not changed in this citation pass.
- **HepG2:** numerical ddPCR results have no figure citation and no selected HepG2 figure is assigned in the index. This is an uncovered result, not an uncited panel in an identified figure.
- **H2452 five-guide results:** still a placeholder and no selected five-guide figure; do not count the initial-guide single panel as covering it.
- **Completion-note drift:** the treatment placeholder still says full comparisons belong in the supplement although the complete comparison was promoted to main F01. The DU145 completion note repeats some results already present. Retain for editorial cleanup rather than treating placeholders as figure citations.
