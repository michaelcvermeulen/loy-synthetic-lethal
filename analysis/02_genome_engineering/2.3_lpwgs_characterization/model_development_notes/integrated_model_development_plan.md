## Parent section

**3.4.6 Generation and genomic characterisation of Y-depleted cancer-cell populations**

Combine model development and lpWGS characterisation in one section organised by cell line. lpWGS establishes the genomic outcome of derivation and therefore belongs beside the editing history of the corresponding model. Within each model's genomic-characterisation account, present lpWGS first, then integrate specimen-matched ddPCR and expression evidence. This retains the earlier lpWGS drafting priority while changing the section architecture.

Open with one short shared account of the pooled-population rationale and model-selection criteria. Explain common assays once in the HT1080 narrative and refer back to them for the other backgrounds. Keep detailed protocols in Chapter3_methods.md and captions in Chapter3_figure_captions.md.
