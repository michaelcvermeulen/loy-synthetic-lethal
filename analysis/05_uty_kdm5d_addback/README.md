# `05_uty_kdm5d_addback/` — Lentiviral *UTY* and *KDM5D* Rescue in Y-Depleted HT1080 Cells

**Thesis Reference:** Chapter 3, Section 3.2.4, Section 3.3.4, and Supplementary Methods S3.4 (`Fig 3.14–3.15`, `Supp Fig 3.42–3.44`).

This module contains the finalized thesis analysis (`THESIS_ADDBACK_2026`) evaluating whether lentiviral re-expression of the Y-linked chromatin regulators ***UTY*** (*KDM6C*, TPR/JmjC H3K27me3 demethylase/COMPASS scaffold) and/or ***KDM5D*** (*JARID1D*, H3K4me2/3 demethylase) reverses the transcriptional and closed-promoter state induced by Y depletion in HT1080 cells.

---

## Experimental Design & Ancestry

- **18 RNA-Seq Libraries across 6 Populations ($n=3$ matched collection occasions per group; `Fig 3.14a`):**
  1. **`sgCTRL`** (May 2026 baseline control population, $n=3$).
  2. **`LOY`** (untransduced Y-depleted `CP + Q53 2x` population, May 2026 baseline, $n=3$).
  3. **`LOY + EV`** (transduced with empty lentiviral vector control, $n=3$).
  4. **`LOY + UTY`** (transduced with *UTY* lentivirus and selected with `G418`, $n=3$).
  5. **`LOY + KDM5D`** (transduced with *KDM5D* lentivirus and selected with `puromycin`, $n=3$).
  6. **`LOY + UTY + KDM5D`** (generated by a second-round *KDM5D* transduction and `puromycin` selection of the established `LOY + UTY` population; $n=3$). Because this combined arm is a direct descendant of the sampled `LOY + UTY` population, comparisons between `LOY + UTY + KDM5D` and `LOY + UTY` include an additional transduction/selection step.

## Headline Findings

1. **Construct Verification & Residual Y Expression (`Fig 3.14b–e`, `Supp Fig 3.42`):**
   - Western blotting (`Fig 3.14b`) and RNA-seq construct quantification (`Fig 3.14c,d`) confirm specific restoration of *UTY* protein and mRNA in `LOY + UTY` and `LOY + UTY + KDM5D`, and *KDM5D* protein and mRNA in `LOY + KDM5D` and `LOY + UTY + KDM5D`.
   - Across all seven non-transduced `Yq` and `Yp` genes (*DDX3Y*, *USP9Y*, *EIF1AY*, *ZFY*, *RPS4Y1*, *PRKY*, *NLGN4Y*), expression remains depleted in all rescue arms, ruling out outgrowth of Y-retaining cells (`Fig 3.14e`).
2. **Partial Reversal of the HT1080 LOY Signature by *UTY*, Not *KDM5D* (`Fig 3.15`, `Supp Fig 3.43`):**
   - Using an independently defined **150-gene batch-1 (August 2025) HT1080 LOY signature** (and verified on the **frozen 100-gene two-window signature**; `Supp Fig 3.43b,c`) to avoid circularity with the May 2026 baseline libraries:
     - **`LOY + UTY` vs. `LOY + EV`** recovers **51.1%** of the composite LOY signature shift ($P = 1.9 \times 10^{-4}$; `Fig 3.15a,b`), with significant directional `fry` rotation tests across both baseline-downregulated (up-rescued) and baseline-upregulated (down-rescued) signature arms (`Fig 3.15c`).
     - **`LOY + UTY + KDM5D` vs. `LOY + EV`** recovers **35.1%** of the composite signature ($P = 0.0031$).
     - **`LOY + KDM5D` vs. `LOY + EV`** shows **no signature recovery** (`-5.5%`, $P = 0.65$), indicating that restoring H3K4me2/3 demethylase *KDM5D* alone is insufficient to reverse the established LOY transcriptional state in HT1080.
3. **Preferential Re-Induction of Closed-Promoter Genes (`Supp Fig 3.43d`, `Supp Fig 3.44`):**
   - Stratifying baseline-downregulated HT1080 genes by their Chapter 3 ATAC-seq promoter status (`code/86_addback_by_atac_class.py`, [`../../figures/chapter3/code/align_addback_atac_current.R`](../../figures/chapter3/code/align_addback_atac_current.R)) demonstrates that *UTY* rescue strongly re-induces genes that underwent concordant transcriptional repression and promoter closing upon Y loss: **36 of 39 joint RNA-down / ATAC-closed genes** increase in expression in `LOY + UTY` relative to `LOY + EV` (`Supp Fig 3.43d`), shifting significantly above matched non-DE controls (`Supp Fig 3.44`).
