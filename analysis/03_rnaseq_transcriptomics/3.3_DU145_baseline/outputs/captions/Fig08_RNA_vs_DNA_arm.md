**Fig08_RNA_vs_DNA_arm**

Autosomal and X chromosome arms with >= 20 mapped genes (CHM13v2.0 arm boundaries). Pearson r = 0.89 at arm level, 0.69 for 10-Mb windows and 0.10 for single genes. The 20p deletion and chrX/15q gains were present in LOY-2X; 20p but not chrX/15q was also seen in DMSO-LOY-1X.
