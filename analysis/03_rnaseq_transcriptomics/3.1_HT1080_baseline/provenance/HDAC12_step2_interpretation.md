# HDAC1/2 step 2 interpretation and computation record

Completed as an authorized extension of the HDAC investigation on 12 September 2026. The configuration and hashes of the completed input objects were written before the first step-2 computation. The analysis is post hoc relative to the original enrichment screen. No new enrichment tests were run and no primary or global enrichment object was refitted.

The HDAC2 source-DOWN arm remains the principal observation: 117 genes average -0.3678 log2 units, versus -0.0186 for the 113-gene UP arm. The 94 DOWN genes absent from the HDAC1 references average -0.4128, with the same sign in both periods; the 19 corresponding UP genes average +0.0480. Catalogue-specific membership does not prove isoform-specific regulation. Source differences do not calibrate enzyme activity.

Top 1/3/5 absolute gene contributions are selected once using the combined effect, with deterministic symbol tie-breaking. For signed pairs, effects are divided by original arm size before ordering; arm-only summaries order absolute gene effects. Removal fractions divide by absolute contributions rather than a cancelling net effect. The five largest HDAC2 DOWN contributors account for 21.36% of absolute effect; the retained arm averages -0.2723. Removing HDAC2 itself has negligible influence. The mean remains negative after abundance restrictions, prior-count changes and RLE normalization.

Existing IFN, ECM/EMT and cell-cycle masks match the earlier chromatin masks exactly. Additional process annotations were declared before computation and do not tune the masks. The 69 HDAC2 DOWN genes outside all six annotation groups average -0.3554. Annotation absence is not biological absence. No chromosome reaches the declared 20% member or absolute-effect concentration threshold; leave-one-chromosome-out was not triggered. No DNA copy-number conclusion follows.

Complete source memberships define signed sample percentiles on the fixed 25,461-gene expressed background. Seven genes shared between HDAC3 UP and DOWN are excluded from both arms. TFPI and TGFB2 conflict across the HDAC1/2/double catalogue but retain their own source-specific directions in original source scores. The 519-member HDAC3 DOWN reference is above the original enrichment maximum: its leading-edge status is missing, not FALSE. Scores are independent of other samples once the common expression filter and representative-locus background have been frozen.

HDAC2 signed score differences are positive in all six matches (+0.546 to +4.442 percentile points) and higher in May; the DOWN arm contributes most of this separation. Removing a match preserves equal period weights. Passage points and omissions are descriptive observations, not independent-population uncertainty. May revisits the same populations, without independent validation.

The eight readouts are post hoc and do not define scores. PLAC8, PPP2R2B, BEX1 and SORL1 decrease at every match under the declared stability and abundance rules. CCND2 and ANTXR2 are source-UP genes that decrease; MCL1 and OSMR meet the near-zero descriptive rule, which is not an equivalence test. Exact IDs, all passing/failing candidate flags and complete evidence are retained. The source audit still lacks the original Senese expression supplement and verified independent array replication.

Interpretation: a persistent partial HDAC2-reference RNA resemblance merits an independent perturbation benchmark. Protein abundance, catalytic activity, chromatin binding, histone acetylation and a causal LOY mechanism remain unmeasured. Steps 3-5 were not executed.
