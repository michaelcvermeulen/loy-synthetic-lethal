# Chromatin-associated signatures: targeted follow-up

This post hoc investigation was requested after the extended MSigDB/CAMERA analysis. The frozen name-based catalogue contains 313 chromatin-related resource sets, including 217 eligible for the original 15-500 represented-gene screen. Mechanistic source-family selection retains 37 sets, including both response arms and non-enriched comparisons; 34 were eligible for original enrichment. BENPORATH_EED_TARGETS, BENPORATH_SUZ12_TARGETS and SENESE_HDAC3_TARGETS_DN exceed 500 represented genes and remain in coverage tables without enrichment claims. The catalogue is a documented keyword screen, not an exhaustive ontology of all chromatin biology.

**Most defensible interpretation:** persistent, selective chromatin-associated target-expression patterns coexist with modest changes in several regulator RNAs. These patterns are not explained solely by the tested matrix/interferon/cell-cycle overlaps, but do not establish a global chromatin state or a unique regulator mechanism.

- HDAC2-knockdown down genes are lower in LOY: original combined NES -2.252, with mean member difference -0.368 log2 units. After excluding the matrix/interferon/cell-cycle reference genes, 92 of 117 genes remain and NES is -2.207. The opposite UP arm changes little (mean -0.019); the signed UP-minus-DN contrast is positive in all six passage matches. This resembles one prominent part of the HDAC2-knockdown response, rather than a complete knockdown phenotype. HDAC1 and HDAC2 RNA are lower by -0.300 and -0.205 log2 units respectively. Protein activity is unmeasured. The double HDAC1/2-knockdown DN signature also persists after exclusions.
- EED-knockdown UP genes are lower: original NES -2.063 and -2.113 after the broadest exclusion (144/154 genes retained). Their mean difference is -0.297, while the EED-knockdown DN genes average +0.032; the signed source-response contrast is negative in all six matches. The May DN arm is nearly unchanged and slightly negative, so a fully reciprocal response is imperfect. This supports an inverse EED-knockdown target pattern, not measured PRC2 activation. EZH2 and SUZ12 RNAs decrease modestly, EED changes little overall, EZH1 increases, and KDM6A/KDM6B change in opposite directions. No protein-complex balance or catalytic activity follows directly from these RNAs.
- Signature names are not reliable perturbation labels: PRC2_EZH2_UP.V1 and PRC1_BMI_UP.V1 actually derive from knockdown experiments in their frozen descriptions. Labels in the figures follow the source experiment. In the DAOY BMI1/PCGF2 series and mouse Suz12-deficient ES-cell series, both response arms generally decrease in HT1080 LOY, limiting any simple activity interpretation.
- Genes with H3K27me3-marked high-CpG promoters in mouse MCV6/MEF source cells are positively enriched in LOY, and this survives the same exclusions. This is compatible with altered expression of those source-defined genes, not evidence that H3K27me3 itself increases in HT1080. Opposing source-signature directions argue for locus/context specificity rather than uniform global repression.
- SMARCE1-expression UP genes decrease, but the DN arm also decreases and SMARCE1 RNA is modestly higher. Thus the result does not establish SMARCE1 loss of function. The KDM5B-expression signatures show no global enrichment and almost zero combined signed response contrast despite lower KDM5B RNA. There is no coherent transcriptional evidence here for generic KDM5-family compensation.
- Broad Reactome chromatin-organization, HDAC, histone-demethylase and PRC2 component sets are not enriched in the combined comparison. Component expression and downstream perturbation signatures answer different questions; neither measures catalytic activity.

Reference masks use the complete frozen Hallmark IFN-alpha/IFN-gamma, Hallmark EMT plus Reactome extracellular-matrix-organization, and Hallmark E2F/G2M/MYC V1/V2 memberships. Genes are removed from both the signature and ranked/voom expression universe. Original TMM exposures and voom weights are retained; CAMERA refits its fixed passage model on retained genes. Five combined-contrast scenarios exclude MMP1/MMP3, IFN, matrix/EMT, IFN plus matrix/EMT, or all reference programs. Focused sensitivities retain nominal P values only, with no replacement of the original all-collection BH family. NES is normalized within each universe; differences between scenario NES values are not calibrated activity changes. These masks do not remove every possible biological overlap and are not causal covariate adjustments.

Source-response arm means are computed on all represented genes, with overlapping UP/DN membership excluded from both arms. They are descriptive mean log2 effects, not enrichment P values or activity scores. Each source-response contrast is also recorded for individual timing matches and leave-one-match-out summaries. Full set memberships, leading-edge annotations, pairwise Jaccard overlaps, chromosome distributions and 34 regulator profiles are exported.

All original CAMERA estimated-correlation limitations remain: no combined global-BH discoveries under that sensitivity, only four residual degrees of freedom, and an independent-residual assumption despite serial sampling. There is one established population per condition and no independent LOY-generation replication. Protein, histone-mark, occupancy, addback and chromatin-perturbation experiments were not performed in this follow-up.

The most discriminating next work is to measure HDAC1/2 and PRC2-related proteins and histone acetylation/H3K27me3, then test selected loci and both response arms after defined perturbations. Compare KDM5D/UTY addback effects under a verified intervention design. Chromatin profiling should include loci outside the fixed matrix/interferon masks, rather than only MMP1/MMP3. Independent populations and matching genomic context are needed to generalize the mechanism.
