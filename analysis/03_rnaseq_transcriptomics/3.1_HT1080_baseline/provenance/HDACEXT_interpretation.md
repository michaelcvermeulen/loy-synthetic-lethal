# Direct public HDAC perturbation benchmark

13 September 2026. Authorized as step 3 of the HDAC investigation. The source is Hess et al. 2022, GSE193885, DOI 10.1371/journal.pgen.1010376. Full methods, numerical tables and figures are in [the report](../outputs/report/HDAC12_external_comparison.html).

The benchmark weakens a specific reduction in HDAC2 catalytic activity as the explanation for LOY. Combined complete-ranking Spearman correlations are 0.068078 for HDAC2 knockout, 0.106319 for active transgene in knockout versus WT, and -0.046474 for inactive versus active transgene. The latter remains negative in both HT1080 periods. Both external response arms often decrease in LOY. PLAC8, PPP2R2B and SORL1 are lower after knockout and after the active-transgene control, but higher in the inactive-versus-active contrast. These are counterexamples to interpreting the earlier partial Senese pattern as a calibrated catalytic-loss assay.

The design and signature rules were frozen before examining HT1080 concordance: 27 source contrasts, 54 top-250-per-arm programs, a 14,881-locus common universe, and four fixed gene-exclusion scenarios. All source qualifiers are exported without the cap as well. BH testing families for the 54 new sets are separate from the original 23,696 MSigDB tests. Estimated-correlation CAMERA has no combined BH < 0.05 result. Fixed-correlation CAMERA and fgsea are explicitly conditional exploratory analyses of the repeated HT1080 populations, with no independent-population claim.

Metadata limits matter. The 69 raw libraries resolve to 63 modeled libraries in 21 groups after excluding six ambiguous MS1/MS3 libraries. S47/S52 are sample-ID strata, not verified batch dates. Author-reported biological triplicates do not demonstrate independent clone generation. HDAC1/2 transgenes use mouse cDNAs with human-reference alignment. HAP_MS was kept as a frozen label-defined comparison, without asserting verified dose/time or equivalence to the published 24-hour worksheet. The poor published match prompted an explicitly separate numerical audit: MS3 is closer, but no treatment identity was assigned from correlation and no model membership was revised. GEO and cached ENA metadata remain insufficient to resolve it.

Eleven fgsea warnings were captured: small-P reporting limits, 0.01% tied May ranks and possible P-value overestimation with unavailable precision estimates. All NES/P results are finite; nine results hit eps=1e-10. Flags and missing log2err values remain in the complete table. External fits use robust edgeR QL (legacy=FALSE), separately normalized within source strata; HT1080 effects and voom inputs are preserved. No gene-independent correlation uncertainty is claimed.

Figure selection was finalized after examining outcomes: all 15 primary contrasts plus all four active-transgene controls, all 54 sets, and the eight pre-existing step-2 readouts. The full 27 contrasts remain visible in the supplement and tables. Neither figure selection nor readouts defined the tested sets. Missing BEX1 external coverage is shown as NA, not zero.

The direct step-3 benchmark is complete. Optional LINCS, complex work and addback remain unrun. A complex/recruitment hypothesis is a future question, not a conclusion of this comparison. No protein abundance, catalytic activity, occupancy or drug sensitivity was measured.
