## 24 September 2026: TF regulators and shared-target TF modules (tf_modules_v1), rules fixed before scoring

The author requested an RNA-only re-analysis of inferred TF regulators, grouped into shared-target modules, for a thesis figure. See PLAN_20260924_tf_modules.md. The 60 v1 CollecTRI/ULM(logFC) hits were already known when these rules were written. The rules below were fixed before any new score was computed and must not be changed after results are seen. Any later deviation is added as a dated note below, with its reason.

- **Inputs:** moderated t from the existing fits. overall = batch_aware_DE batch_condition combined; AUG_2025, MAY_2026, interaction and CTRL_vs_parental come from programme_analysis_v1 DE.rds. logFC is a secondary input only. Unique-symbol mapping, gene universe (25,526 autosomal/non-PAR X) and exposures are unchanged. Original outputs are read-only and hash-checked.
- **Priors:**
  - Primary: CollecTRI, retrieved through OmnipathR 3.18.4 on 24 Sep 2026 (split_complexes=FALSE; loaded from the OmniPath cache; 43,159 edges; hashed and diffed against the frozen v1 file).
  - Alternatives for R4: DoRothEA confidence A-C (dorothea 1.22), and TFActProfiler_all.csv from github HikaruSugimoto/tfactprofiler at commit 213e0d73e3 (BSD-3; source/target/weight).
  - TFActProfiler_fibroblast.csv is a sensitivity analysis only and does not enter R4.
  - Conflicting duplicate edges are removed as in v1. Self-edges (TF targeting its own gene) are kept and flagged.
- **Methods:** decoupleR 2.16 ULM, MLM, WSUM (norm_wsum, 1,000 permutations, seed 42) and VIPER (analytic, pleiotropy=FALSE), all with minsize 5. Significance family: ULM(t) with BH across TFs per contrast (unchanged v1 family). The consensus score is the mean over methods of sign(score) x qnorm(1 - p/2), used for ranking and display only; p-values are never combined across methods. limma::fry with signed mor weights on the batch_condition voom is reported per regulon as a flag that accounts for correlation between genes; it is not part of the rule.
- **Robust regulator rule:**
  - R1: overall ULM(t) FDR < 0.05.
  - R2: same sign in AUG_2025 and MAY_2026 (ULM(t) score).
  - R3: at least 3 of 4 methods have the same sign as ULM and nominal p < 0.05 (overall t).
  - R4: same ULM(t) sign under each alternative prior in which the TF has at least 5 targets. A prior that doesn't cover the TF counts as not applicable, not as a failure.
  - R5: same sign in all 6 leave-one-passage-pair-out refits of the batch + condition model (10 libraries each).
  - R6: after removing the self-edge and the 3 targets contributing most (contribution = mor x t x sign(score)), ULM(t) keeps its sign with p < 0.05.
  - Tiers: robust = R1-R6 all true; supported = R1 and R2 plus at least 3 of R3-R6. These filters reuse the same reads; they measure stability, not independent replication.
- **Overlap and redundancy:**
  - Leading contributors are the fewest top-ranked positive-contribution targets that together make up 80% of the positive contribution.
  - Concentration flag: the top 3 targets make up more than 50% of the positive contribution.
  - Unique-target ULM is run for TFs with at least 5 unique targets within the candidate set.
  - Copy-number flag: at least 50% of leading contributors lie on one chromosome arm, or the sign/p < 0.05 is lost after removing genes on arms with a non-diploid lpWGS state.
- **Modules:**
  - Nodes: robust regulators, or robust plus supported if fewer than 8 are robust.
  - Similarity: weighted overlap coefficient on leading-contributor targets, S_AB = sum over shared targets of min(c_A, c_B) / min(sum c_A, sum c_B).
  - Clustering: average-linkage hierarchical on 1 - S, with k = argmax mean silhouette over 2..floor(n/2). Singletons are unassigned.
  - Stability: 1,000 gene bootstraps and 200 random 20% edge drops. A module is retained at mean co-clustering Jaccard >= 0.6 and called stable at >= 0.75.
  - Sensitivities: Leiden (resolution sweep, ARI), Jaccard similarity, and similarity restricted to the 662 consistent genes.
  - Nulls: 200 degree-preserving rewired CollecTRI networks (null count of R1-R2 regulators, null silhouette); size-matched random TF sets (overlap level); size- and expression-matched gene-set permutation plus fry on each module's signed target union.
  - Co-activity (per-library ULM with batch and condition removed) and CollecTRI TF->TF edges are supporting evidence only and do not define modules.
- **Confounding programmes:** Hallmark IFN-alpha/gamma, E2F/G2M, TNFA/NF-kB, P53, UPR and hypoxia, plus the van den Brink 2017 dissociation/immediate-early genes. For each module, report driver overlap and re-score with each programme's genes removed. A module is confounder-dependent if the module-union ULM loses its sign or p < 0.05. Drift flag: a robust TF with the same sign and p < 0.05 in CTRL_vs_parental.
- **Outputs:** outputs/tf_modules_v1/. v1 programme_analysis outputs and figures are unchanged. The thesis figure is only a candidate until the author selects it.
