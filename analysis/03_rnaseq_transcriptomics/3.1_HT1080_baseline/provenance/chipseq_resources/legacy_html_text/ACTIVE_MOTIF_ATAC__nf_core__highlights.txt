5. Motif enrichment — TFAP2 family dominates the LOY-lost set
Going in we predicted NF-κB / IRF / STAT to dominate the LOY-lost set
(immune-evasion narrative). The actual result:
TFAP2 family
sweeps the top hits at padj ≈ 1e-23 to 1e-35
. AP-2
transcription factors regulate mesenchymal / neural-crest /
fibroblast-lineage programs — consistent with matrisome / EMT signal in
step 06’s DOWN-CLOSED ORA. Mechanistic working story:
loss of
fibrosarcoma lineage identity
rather than immune evasion (at
least in HT1080).
Top motifs enriched in LOY-LOST DARs (all peaks)
The lost side is far more trustworthy than gained: gained DARs are
dominated by chr18q CNV-bearing peaks (see step 7), so gained-side motif
signal may be sequence-content artefact of chr18 gene density.

7. CNV check — chr18q clonal gain in LOY
LOY HT1080 carries a clonal
chr18q copy gain
that
confounds the gained-side signal. Step 7 quantifies the shift across 1
Mb bins of ATAC coverage.
chr18 whole-chromosome mean log2(LOY/CTRL): 0.294
chr18q21 (46-60 Mb) mean log2(LOY/CTRL):    0.432
chr18 outside q21 mean log2(LOY/CTRL):      0.265
chrY mean (expected highly negative for LOY): -1.388

VERDICT: ambiguous — chr18q21 differs from rest-of-chr18 mean by 0.10–0.20 log2 units. Worth a deeper look but not a clear CNV.
