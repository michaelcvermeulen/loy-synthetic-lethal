# PRC2, H3K27me3 and epigenetic GSEA investigation

Authorized 14 September 2026. Build on the original MSigDB 2026.1.Hs analysis and completed chromatin/HDAC follow-ups. The core remains 12 repeated libraries from one established population per condition and one shared generation history. Original counts, expression models, global enrichment tests and earlier figure exports remain frozen.

1. Expand the documented name-based epigenetic catalogue across all available collections, including PRC1/2, histone marks, methylation, remodelers and acetylation regulators. Freeze same-release descriptions. Retain all coverage failures, negative results and original 23,696-set global BH values. The catalogue is not an exhaustive ontology or a new testing family.
2. Audit source semantics: EED/EZH2/SUZ12 depletion responses, occupancy in source cells, promoter histone-mark annotations, differential marks in cancer, genetic perturbation, combined drug exposure, component pathways and computational associations are different evidence types. Check primary sources and record shared publications and cell context.
3. Examine complete PRC2 response arms, all six passage matches, sample scores and leave-one-match-out summaries. Partition source membership across EED/EZH2/SUZ12; compare EED-responsive genes with the MEF/MCV6 H3K27me3-marked gene union. Inspect shared and distinct genes without assigning a chromatin mark to HT1080 from another cell type.
4. Quantify gene influence, abundance, prior-count/normalization stability, nucleosome membership and the existing IFN/matrix/cycle overlaps. Fixed gene removals yield descriptive means and counts, with no additional significance tests. Select a balanced post hoc readout panel using the explicit configuration; export every eligible and rejected candidate.
5. Compare histone-mark annotations across source studies, cells and CpG classes, retaining unavailable and size-ineligible sets. Place PRC2 alongside PRC1, H3K4/bivalent marks, H3K9, DNA methylation, HDAC and remodeler results. Separate regulator RNA from target expression and biochemical activity.
6. Produce a focused self-contained report, two publication figures, captions and per-panel source data. Integrate the report and figures into the existing deliverables. Validate scientific contracts, exact original-result preservation, figure labels/fonts/resolution and report links; visually review color and color-vision simulations.

This is a deep analysis of the existing RNA-seq/GSEA evidence. It does not reprocess a new external PRC2 perturbation dataset or measure HT1080 chromatin marks. Any proposed protein, chromatin or external-perturbation validation will be identified as future work.

Completed analysis: 339 catalogue sets, 235 original eligible tests, 94 focused sets (79 eligible), complete source-arm and gene diagnostics, and a 41-set promoter-state atlas. The EED program and MEF/MCV6 source-mark union overlap by only six genes. Figure 10 and Figure 11 add eight panels; [the focused report](outputs/report/epigenetic_deep_dive.html) documents interpretation and limitations. No external PRC2 model or chromatin measurement was added.
