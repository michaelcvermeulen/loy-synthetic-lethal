**Figure 8 | Genes underlying the HDAC-associated expression pattern.** **a**, Mutually exclusive memberships across the Senese HDAC1, HDAC2 and double-knockdown references, restricted to represented HT1080 genes. Single-isoform-only labels mean absence from the other single-knockdown list; double-knockdown overlap is allowed. Any directional conflict takes precedence. HDAC3-only comparison genes are omitted here. **b**, All six matched differences in signed source-UP-minus-source-DN percentile scores. Each sample ranks its counts on the same 25,461 expressed representative genes; pp denotes percentile points. **c**, Eight post hoc readouts selected by the recorded abundance, prior-count and temporal-stability rules, including source-UP genes with opposite or near-zero responses. A1-A3 and M1-M3 denote August and May passages. These genes do not define the program scores. **d**, Combined mean expression contrasts after fixed exclusions. Top contributors are ranked by absolute arm-size-weighted combined effects and held fixed across periods. Retained arm means are recomputed. Complete UP and DN summaries accompany every diagnostic. Within-source ambiguous UP/DN genes are excluded from signed scores; this removes seven HDAC3 genes from both arms. Reference differences do not calibrate isoform activity. The 12 libraries repeatedly sample one population per condition from one generation history. All summaries are descriptive; no new P values, inferential intervals or independent validation are claimed. Protein activity and direct target regulation remain unmeasured.
