**Figure 7 | Chromatin-associated expression signatures and their specificity.** **a**, Original enrichment for 12 selected signatures; names describe the source experiment, not regulator activity in HT1080. KD denotes knockdown. **b**, Mean combined expression differences for both response arms of ten source perturbations. UP and DN refer to the source response; opposite directions support a more coherent perturbation-like pattern than a decrease of both arms. **c**, Percentage of represented signature genes shared with fixed interferon, matrix/EMT and cell-cycle reference sets, defined without selecting on LOY effects. **d**, Combined fgsea after excluding reference genes from both the signature and ranked universe. Each NES is normalized within its own universe and is not an activity difference across scenarios. All 37 focused set definitions, eligible and ineligible sets, original global BH values, CAMERA results and five exclusion sensitivities are exported. Original tests retain BH across all 23,696 eligible MSigDB sets; focused sensitivities report nominal P values without confirmatory FDR claims. H3K27me3 sets describe marked genes in mouse source cells mapped to human genes; they do not measure histone marks in HT1080. The 12 libraries repeatedly sample one established population per condition from one generation history. CAMERA assumes independent residuals after passage fixed effects; estimated-correlation sensitivity remains inconclusive. No protein activity or chromatin-occupancy claim is established.
