**Figure 10 | Distinct PRC2-related expression programs in established HT1080 populations.** **a**, Complete represented UP and DN arms from EED, EZH2 and SUZ12 knockdowns in human TIG3 fibroblasts (Bracken et al., 2006). Values are mean matched log2 expression differences, with equal period weights for the combined estimate. **b**, Matched changes in mean UP-minus-DN within-sample percentile scores on the unchanged 25,461-gene background. A1-A3 and M1-M3 denote August and May passages. Symbols identify source knockdowns; these are descriptive scores, not measured PRC2 activity. **c**, Complete membership partitions of the EED-knockdown UP set and the union of the Mikkelsen MEF and MCV6 high-CpG H3K27me3 promoter sets; counts are shown in parentheses. These marks were measured in mouse source cells. **d**, Post hoc examples from EED-only (E) and mark-only (M) partitions, including higher and lower genes. Selection requires mean CPM >= 2, absolute combined difference >= 0.5, concordant periods, all six omission estimates and both normalization sensitivities. Up to three genes per direction and partition are selected by absolute effect; only one higher EED-only gene qualifies. Values are unscaled matched effects. Colors are centered on zero with panel-specific scales. All 12 libraries repeatedly sample one established population per condition from shared generation history. No gene-independent intervals, new enrichment tests or independent-population validation are implied.
