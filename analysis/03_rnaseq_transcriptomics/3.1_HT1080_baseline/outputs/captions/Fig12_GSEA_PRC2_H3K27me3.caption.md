**Figure 12 | PRC2 depletion-response and source H3K27me3 promoter gene sets.** **a-d**, Source RNA responses to EED, EZH2 or SUZ12 knockdown in human TIG3 fibroblasts; both EED response arms and the weaker SUZ12 result are retained. **e,f**, Genes annotated with H3K27me3 at high-CpG promoters in mouse MEF and partially reprogrammed MCV6 source cells. Each panel shows the exact weighted running enrichment score (ES; exponent 1), all set-member hit positions and the ranked log2 expression differences. The 25,461 representative genes are ordered by combined LOY-minus-sgCTRL effect, from higher on the left to lower on the right. The colored curve, shaded interval and colored hits identify enrichment direction and leading-edge region; gray hits mark remaining members. A dot and dashed segment locate the ES extremum. All panels share ES and ranked-effect scales. Annotations reproduce original normalized enrichment scores (NES), global BH values across 23,696 tested sets and represented-gene counts. The combined effect equally weights August and May; 12 libraries repeatedly sample one established population per condition. Display selection is post hoc; no new enrichment tests were run. Estimated-correlation CAMERA yields no combined global-BH discoveries. Source signatures do not measure HT1080 enzyme activity or chromatin marks.
