**Figure 1 | Sampling design and count-level quality control.** **a**, Principal-component analysis of 25,526 expressed autosomal and non-PAR X genes, using TMM-normalized log2 CPM (prior count 2), centered by gene without variance scaling. **b**, Assigned reads divided by all reads in featureCounts summaries; all 15 libraries are shown. **c**, Pearson correlations of core normalized expression. Codes denote August (A) or May (M), sgCTRL (C), LOY (L), or parental (P), and passage. **d**, Confirmed sampling structure; spacing indicates order, not elapsed time. The core comprises 12 RNA libraries: one established sgCTRL and one LOY population, each measured at three successive passages in both periods, from one shared nucleofection origin. Passage/day matches are within period. Numbers label passages; circles denote August and triangles May. Blue denotes sgCTRL, vermilion LOY, and gray parental. Three August parental libraries provide context and do not enter core normalization or effects. No samples were excluded. All panels are descriptive; libraries and passage matches are not independent population-generation replicates. Source data accompany panels a-d.
