**Figure 6 | ZMYND8 inference is driven by a small target subset.** **a**, ZMYND8, KDM5C and KDM5D expression across all 12 core libraries; y-axis scales differ by gene. Lines connect passage order within population and period, without implying known elapsed time. **b**, ZMYND8 ULM scores from the combined, August and May effect signatures using all eight expressed CollecTRI targets. Positive scores indicate agreement with the signed regulon, not measured ZMYND8 abundance or biochemical activity. **c**, All eight targets across six timing matches, ordered by absolute combined signed effect. Expression differences are multiplied by activating (+) or repressing (-) edge signs. A1-A3 and M1-M3 denote August and May. **d**, Post hoc removal of MMP1, MMP3 or both regulon edges while preserving the complete expression background; the full single-target omission series is exported. These are influence diagnostics, not independently validated reduced regulons. Combined-signature ZMYND8 RNA changes little, while MMP1 and MMP3 decreases dominate the positive target pattern. Original target-pattern P values and BH corrections across all regulators are in source data. There is one established population per condition and one shared generation origin; repeated passages do not establish biological replication. No claim of increased ZMYND8 protein activity, KDM5C compensation, chromatin binding or metastatic behavior follows from these RNA measurements.
