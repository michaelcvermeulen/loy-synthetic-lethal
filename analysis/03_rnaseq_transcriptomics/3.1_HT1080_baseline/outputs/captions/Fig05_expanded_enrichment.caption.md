**Figure 5 | Expanded gene-set enrichment and CAMERA.** **a**, Available and eligible MSigDB H and C1-C9 sets; eligibility requires 15-500 represented genes in the common expressed autosomal/non-PAR X symbol universe. **b**, Combined-contrast sets with BH-adjusted P <0.05 for each method. BH spans all eligible sets across all ten collections, separately per method and contrast. **c**, Comparison of direction-signed -log10 global BH values; positive denotes higher expression association in LOY. Values are capped at 12 for display; full values accompany source data. Overlapping sets are not independent discoveries. **d**, Voom mean-variance observations and fitted trend. fgsea tests the full equal-period-weight effect ranking. CAMERA uses raw counts, existing TMM exposures, voom weights, six passage/day block intercepts and two period-specific LOY effects; the combined contrast weights these effects equally. CAMERA uses two-sided competitive tests, fixed inter-gene correlation 0.01, and four model residual degrees of freedom; estimated-correlation sensitivities are exported separately. All 12 libraries sample one established population per condition from one generation history. CAMERA assumes independent residuals after fixed effects and does not model residual serial dependence. Its exploratory P values do not establish population replication, causal LOY effects or pathway activity.
