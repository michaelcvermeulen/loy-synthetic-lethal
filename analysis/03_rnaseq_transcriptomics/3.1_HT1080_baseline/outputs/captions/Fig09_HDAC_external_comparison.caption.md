**Figure 9 | Public HDAC perturbations do not establish a specific HDAC2 catalytic-loss state in LOY.** **a**, Spearman correlations between HAP1 perturbation and HT1080 effect rankings on 14,881 common loci. All 15 primary contrasts and all four active-transgene controls are shown. A slash denotes numerator versus denominator; CI denotes catalytically inactive transgene, with its cellular background in parentheses. **b**, Combined HT1080 means of separately defined source UP and DN arms, in the same row order. Sets use external nonzero-effect BH < 0.05 and absolute log2 fold change >= 0.5, capped at 250 genes per arm by QL F before overlap filtering. **c**, Exploratory support across all 54 sets; dashed lines mark secondary BH = 0.05. Labels identify HDAC2 knockout and CI-versus-active arms. The estimated-correlation sensitivity has no combined BH < 0.05 result. **d**, Eight previously selected readouts, with unscaled effects. External columns are versus WT except CI/active. Gray NA indicates absent common-background coverage, not zero expression. HAP1 models use 63 libraries in two sample-ID strata, with author-reported culture triplicates of established lines; independent clone replication is unverified. MS-275 label MS has unresolved dose/time metadata. HDAC1/2 transgenes are mouse cDNAs aligned to a human reference. HT1080 repeatedly samples one population per condition from a shared generation history. Correlations and arm means are descriptive. CAMERA assumes independent residuals; neither method establishes enzyme activity, independent-population validation or drug sensitivity. Full 27-contrast results accompany the figure.
