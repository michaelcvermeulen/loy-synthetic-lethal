**Supplementary Figure 3 | Matched program-score diagnostics.** **a**, LOY-minus-sgCTRL differences in mean within-sample percentile ranks for eight prespecified Hallmark programs. The rank background contains all core eligible, uniquely mapped genes; scores are computed independently within each sample. **b**, Matched PROGENy footprint differences using the human top-100 model. Represented gene weights are divided by their absolute sum within each footprint, then applied to normalized log2 expression differences. Thus these are weighted expression summaries, not standardized PROGENy activity units. All six passage/day matches are shown, with separate symmetric color scales for the two scoring methods. The 12 libraries represent repeated measurements of one sgCTRL and one LOY population from one shared nucleofection origin. No group-level significance tests or inferential intervals are used. Cell-cycle, interferon and stress signatures are biological diagnostics and were not automatically regressed out. Exact gene membership, footprint weights, target coverage and matched scores are supplied with the source tables.
