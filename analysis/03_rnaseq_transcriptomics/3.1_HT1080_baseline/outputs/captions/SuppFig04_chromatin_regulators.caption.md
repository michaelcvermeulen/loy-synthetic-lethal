**Supplementary Figure 4 | RNA expression of chromatin regulators.** **a**, All 32 mechanism-selected autosomal or X-linked regulators across six matched passages; colors show original TMM-normalized log2 CPM differences with prior count 2, without row scaling. **b**, UTY and KDM5D use a separate quantitative axis because their depletion is much larger than the other RNA differences. A1-A3 and M1-M3 denote August and May matches. Regulator selection follows the mechanistic questions, without significance or magnitude filtering. Gene-level effects, raw counting identities, observed passage ranges and leave-one-match-out summaries accompany the full tables. RNA differences do not measure protein abundance, complex assembly, nuclear localization, histone methylation/acetylation or catalytic activity. Both periods sample the same two established populations from one generation history. No gene-level P values or independent-population uncertainty intervals are reported.
