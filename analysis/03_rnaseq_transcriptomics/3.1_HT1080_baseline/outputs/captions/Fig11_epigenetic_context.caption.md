**Figure 11 | Epigenetic enrichments depend on source context and response direction.** **a**, All available Mikkelsen/Meissner promoter-state combinations in the 339-set screen, organized by study, source cell and high/intermediate/low CpG class (HCP/ICP/LCP). Values are original combined fgsea normalized enrichment scores (NES); * marks original global BH < 0.05 across 23,696 sets. Gray NT denotes overlap outside 15-500 genes; a dash denotes an unavailable combination. H3 prefixes are shortened to K. MCV6 is partially reprogrammed; IPS denotes source induced pluripotent cells. **b**, Complete UP and DN source RNA-arm means. KD denotes knockdown. The KDM1A publication describes loss responses, whereas its MSigDB comparison wording reverses the genotype order; labels are preserved without activity calibration. **c**, Mean combined effects after removing the frozen interferon/matrix/cycle union, nucleosome genes, the five or ten largest absolute effects, or genes with mean CPM < 5. Removals are fixed across periods; these are descriptive means, not recalculated enrichment tests. **d**, Selected regulator transcripts across periods and their equally weighted mean; the complete regulator table accompanies the figure. Heatmaps use separate zero-centered scales. Source chromatin states and RNA responses do not establish HT1080 H3K27me3, H3K9me3, DNA methylation, complex abundance or enzyme activity. Conditional enrichment statistics remain exploratory because the 12 HT1080 libraries repeatedly sample the same established populations; estimated-correlation CAMERA yields no combined global BH < 0.05 result.
