**Figure 2 | Expression differences and their persistence across sampling periods.** **a**, Equal-period-weighted mean of six passage-matched LOY-minus-sgCTRL log2 CPM differences versus mean abundance. Dashed lines mark an absolute difference of 0.5, a descriptive magnitude threshold. **b**, Mean differences in August and May; the diagonal marks equality. **c**, Individual normalized expression trajectories for the four largest absolute candidate effects. Lines connect successive passages within population and period. **d**, Six matched differences for each of the top 12 candidates; gray lines span observed minima and maxima, and black diamonds mark equal-period means. Displayed candidates are protein-coding genes with mean CPM at least 2, absolute mean difference at least 0.5, concordant period directions and no direction reversal after any single-match deletion, ordered by absolute mean effect. Circles denote August and triangles May; expression colors denote sgCTRL (blue) and LOY (vermilion). In a-b, colors indicate effect direction beyond the magnitude threshold; gray indicates smaller effects. Counts use TMM exposure offsets and log2 CPM with prior count 2. Twelve libraries represent repeated sampling of one population per condition from one origin. Observed ranges are not confidence intervals. No gene-level significance test is used. Source data accompany panels a-d.
