**Supplementary Figure 1 | Contextual samples and count composition.** **a**, PCA of all 15 libraries using its own TMM normalization, with three parental libraries in gray. **b**, Core PCA after removal of an additive sampling-period term while preserving treatment; these values are used only for visualization. **c**, Mitochondrial, ribosomal-protein and top-ten-gene fractions of assigned counts. Lines follow passage order within period and group. **d**, Detected genes versus assigned read depth. PCA uses the core eligible gene set, centered log2 CPM with prior count 2 and no variance scaling. Blue denotes sgCTRL, vermilion LOY, circles August and triangles May. Twelve core libraries represent one established population per condition sampled three times per period across two periods, from one generation history. Parental origins and matching are not established; they do not enter core effects. Period removal can subtract biological drift as well as technical differences and is not used in downstream effect estimates. Count composition is descriptive and supplies no automatic sample-exclusion rule. Source data accompany panels a-d.
