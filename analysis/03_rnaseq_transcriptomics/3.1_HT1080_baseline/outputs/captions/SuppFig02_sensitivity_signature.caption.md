**Supplementary Figure 2 | Effect sensitivity and an August-derived signature.** **a**, TMM versus RLE normalization on the same expressed background. **b**, Log2 CPM prior count 2 versus 0.25 under identical TMM offsets. Diagonals mark equal estimates. **c**, Largest absolute change in the equal-period mean after deleting any single passage match; the two periods retain equal weight after deletion. **d**, A signature selected using August alone, with 50 higher and 50 lower genes. August-specific filtering, normalization and symbol selection precede selection by absolute mean difference (at least 0.5), with all three passage differences in the same direction and a maximum of 50 genes per direction. The scoring background is frozen from August. Each sample is scored independently as mean within-sample percentile rank of higher genes minus that of lower genes. May observations never determine signature membership or scoring background. Blue denotes sgCTRL, vermilion LOY, circles August and triangles May. Lines follow passage order. Twelve libraries repeatedly sample the same two established populations from one generation history. May therefore assesses temporal persistence, not validation in independently generated populations. All panels are descriptive. Full gene/filter and signature source tables accompany the figure.
