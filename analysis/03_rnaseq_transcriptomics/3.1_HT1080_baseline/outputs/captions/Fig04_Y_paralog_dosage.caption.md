**Figure 4 | Residual Y-linked RNA and paralog abundance.** **a**, Summed normalized CPM for non-PAR Yp, non-PAR Yq, X PAR and Y PAR features, shown as log2(sum CPM + 1). Lines connect passages within each population and period; panel scales differ. **b**, Individual gene-level log2 CPM for eight prespecified Y genes. Sample codes follow Figure 1. **c**, All six matched log2 differences for the X partners KDM6A, KDM5C, DDX3X and EIF1AX. **d**, Six passage-matched X+Y relative-abundance proxies for each prespecified pair. Each gene abundance is CPM divided by its featureCounts union-exon length in kilobases; pair ratios are (X_LOY + Y_LOY)/(X_sgCTRL + Y_sgCTRL), then log2 transformed. The vertical line marks an unchanged total proxy. Small vertical jitter separates measurements and has no biological meaning. All branches carry the same core TMM exposure offsets; Y genes are not independently normalized. GRCh38 cytobands define arms and pinned PAR intervals define PAR features. Blue denotes sgCTRL, vermilion LOY; circles denote August and triangles May. Twelve libraries repeatedly sample one population per condition from one origin. These count-derived proxies depend on annotation, isoform composition and mapping specificity. They do not establish protein compensation, Y retention, residual Y-positive cells or independent biological replication. No inferential error bars or gene-level tests are used. Source data include counts, feature lengths and denominator-support flags.
