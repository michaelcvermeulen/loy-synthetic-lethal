**Figure 3 | Exploratory pathway and regulator patterns.** **a**, Ten Hallmark pathways ordered by combined-signature BH-adjusted enrichment P value; symbols show combined, August and May NES. **b**, Ten CollecTRI regulators ordered by combined-signature BH-adjusted target-pattern P value. **c**, Up to 14 unique leading-edge genes from the first two pathways, ordered by absolute combined effect; colors show all six matched log2 differences. **d**, Up to 14 unique targets of the first two regulators, ordered by absolute signed combined effect. Target differences are multiplied by the activating (+) or repressing (-) edge sign; their regulator assignments are in source data. Heatmap limits are shared and symmetric; A1-A3 and M1-M3 denote August and May matches. fgsea uses all eligible uniquely mapped genes ranked by effect, sets of 15-500 represented genes, signed enrichment, and minimum reported P value 1e-10. Primary BH correction spans all eligible H and C1-C9 sets within each contrast. CollecTRI ULM requires at least five represented targets and uses two-sided target-pattern P values, BH-adjusted across regulators within contrast. Exact statistics, coverage and leading edges accompany source data. These conditional tests characterize gene-set or target patterns; they do not supply independent population replication. The 12 libraries repeatedly sample one sgCTRL and one LOY population from one origin. NES values are enrichment summaries, not absolute pathway activities.
