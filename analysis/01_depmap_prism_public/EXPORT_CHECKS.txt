   - figure_source_data/: all 46 files byte-identical to the stored ones, in both runs.
   - Figures 1, 3, 4, 7, 9, 10, 11, 13 and 14: PNG and SVG byte-identical to the stored

     size to within two bytes. A PDF carries a date stamp; the difference was not

   10 were drawn on 2026-10-03, Figures 11 and 12 on 2026-10-04, Figure 13 on
